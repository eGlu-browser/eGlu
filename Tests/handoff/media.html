Tides explained in four minutes

Harbour School, 18,204 views

Why the sea rises and falls twice a day, shown with a bucket of water and a lamp for the moon. Made for the sailing class at the harbour school, and for anyone who has been told that the moon pulls the sea and wondered why there are then two high waters a day and not one.

Transcript

0:00 The sea rises and falls twice a day, and here is why.

0:21 This lamp is the moon. The bucket is the sea, and I am the earth.

1:04 The water nearest the lamp is pulled most, and heaps up.

1:52 The water on the far side is pulled least, and is left behind.

2:40 So there are two heaps, and the earth turns through both in a day.

3:35 When the sun pulls along the same line, the heaps are higher: a spring tide.

## Transcript

The sea rose and fell twice a day, and here is why. The lamp on the headland is the moon of this small coast. A ferry leaves the quay at six and comes back with the mail, and gulls follow it out and ride it in. The harbour café opens at seven, and the fishermen talk over the morning's catch. By noon the tide is out and the boats sit on the mud. The old pier was rebuilt after the storm of ninety-eight, and the bell on the chapel is still rung by hand. Everyone on the front knows the words the fog horn says.

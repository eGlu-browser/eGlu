Harbour wall holds through the night

By R. Quill, 3 January 2026

The harbour wall at North Quay held through the night's storm, engineers said on Friday, after waves broke over the pier for nine hours.

Crews walked the length of the wall at first light. They found cracked coping stones at the east end and a fishing boat lifted onto the lower steps, but no breach.

"The wall did what it was built to do," said M. Ahern, the port's chief engineer. "The boat is a different matter."

Repairs begin on Monday. The ferry timetable is unchanged.

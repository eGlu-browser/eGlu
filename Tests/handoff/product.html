Tide clock, brass

129 EUR, in stock

A tide clock has one hand, which goes round once in twelve hours and twenty-five minutes, the time from one high water to the next. Set it at high water on a day of full moon and it will show the hours to the next high and the next low for weeks, on any coast where the tide comes twice a day.

Specifications

CaseBrass, lacquered
Across18 cm
MovementQuartz, one AA cell
Made inDover

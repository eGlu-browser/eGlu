# Relay

Relay passes messages between a prototype on a screen and the things around it: a steering wheel, a game pad, a board of sensors. A designer uses it to try out how a product answers to the hand, before anyone has built the product.

## Problem

![One desk, one machine, and a team standing round it](https://studio.example/images/relay-before.png)
Old Relay

> **Constraints in the old product**
>
> Relay was why large customers renewed, and it had not been changed in years.

"My teammate had built the setup on her machine, and I built all of it again on mine."

## New approach

> **One thing to keep a whole setup in**
>
> I proposed a Stage: its devices, its wiring and its layout, kept as one thing that can be opened, shared and copied.

The old flow beside the new

## Improvement

![The console, with a row for each message](https://studio.example/images/relay-console.png)
Filter

[Video: A row opens to show the whole message]
New Relay

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| /record | Keeps every message from now until it is stopped. |
| /replay | Sends a kept run of messages again, at the pace they came. |

> The console lists each message with its time, its value and where it came from.

## Outcome

The work shipped in June 2026 to the first six teams. Within a month four of them had moved their setups to shared Stages, and the questions to support about lost configurations stopped.

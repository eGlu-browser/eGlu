# Design together

Forma is where teams design, prototype and hand off in one place.

[Start for free](https://forma.example/signup)

## Everything a design team needs

- Frames that lay themselves out as their contents change.

- Components with variants, kept in one shared library.

- Prototypes that run on a phone as they are drawn.

- Comments pinned to the place they are about.

- A history of every file, back to its first day.

- Branches for trying a change without touching the original.

- Colour and type styles that follow a file's theme.

- Measurements and code for whoever builds the design.

- Vector drawing with a pen that edits curves in place.

- Plugins written in the language of the web.

- Exports to PNG, SVG and PDF at any size.

- Cursors that show who is working where, as they work.

## How teams use it

A designer at a company of forty opens the file a product manager started the evening before, finds the three frames that were sketched in boxes and grey text, and replaces them with components from the team's library; by noon the engineer who will build the screen has left six comments on it, each pinned to the control it is about.

Nothing was exported and nothing was sent.

## Integrations

Forma works with the tools a team already has.

- Tracker: link a frame to the issue it answers.

- Chat: a message when someone comments on your file.

- Repository: design tokens written to a branch as JSON.

- Docs: a live frame inside a page of writing.

- Calendar: a review booked from the file it is about.

- Storage: a copy of every file in the team's own bucket.

- Sign-in: the team's own directory decides who gets in.

- Analytics: which components are used, and where.

- Translation: every string in a file sent out and brought back.

- Whiteboard: a sketch becomes frames with one command.

- Presenter: a prototype shown full screen, with notes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I bring my files from another tool?

Yes. Forma opens Sketch and Figma files, and keeps their components and styles.

### What happens to my files if I stop paying?

They stay, read-only, for as long as the account does. You can export them at any time.

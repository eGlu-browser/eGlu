# What eGlu takes from Search's extensions

eGlu's Chrome extension support is written fresh, shaped for spaces from the start (Docs/ExtensionsDesign.md, decided 21 October 2026). Search, the Mac browser eGlu began as a fork of (github.com/driceroland/Search, MIT, on this repository's `upstream` remote), found most of what goes wrong with Chrome extensions on WebKit first. Each of its commits about extensions is read before the part of eGlu it touches is written, and marked here:

- **Taken**: the fix is in eGlu, as it was.
- **Taken, changed**: the fix is in eGlu, changed for spaces or for Chromium's rules; how is said.
- **Not needed**: it doesn't apply, and why (Search's private tabs, its one window, its bench).
- **Not read yet**.

New upstream commits are added as they land: `git log upstream/main -i --grep=extension` and the commits touching Search's extension files.

Area is from the files a commit touched; "Elsewhere" means only Search's other files (its tabs, its browser, its README). Listed oldest first, 156 commits, as of `upstream/main` b81932c (20 October 2026).

# Privacy Policy

**Safience** for iPhone and iPad · Effective October 21, 2026

Safience is a web browser made by Taehee Jung. This policy says what the app does with your information. In short: **Safience collects nothing.** It has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What it keeps stays on your device, or in your own iCloud account if you turn on iCloud sync.

## What Safience collects

Nothing. The developer receives no data from the app: not what you browse, not what you search, not how you use the app. There is no sign-up, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code from anyone.

## What stays on your device

To work as a browser, the app keeps some things on your iPhone or iPad. They are not sent to the developer or anyone else.

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Your spaces, their tabs and their bookmarks | So your tabs and bookmarks are there the next time you open the app |
| Your settings | So the app works the way you set it |
| Your browsing history: the pages you go to in each space, when, their titles and what they say they are about (their description), and the searches you typed in the address bar | So you can find a page again in History and the address bar. Each space keeps its own. Kept for a month unless you change it in Settings › History, and cleared there or from History's Clear. It leaves the device only if you turn on History in Settings › iCloud (below). Never kept: sign-in pages, a page that asks for a password, an address that carries a sign-in, and a page opened in a pop-up |
| Each space's website data: cookies, sign-ins and what sites store | So you stay signed in to the sites you use. Each space keeps its own, apart from the others. WebKit, Apple's web engine, keeps this data; the app itself never reads your cookies |
| Pictures of tabs that are put to sleep to save memory | So a sleeping tab looks the way you left it while it loads again. A sign-in page is never saved as a picture |
| Sites' icons | To show next to tabs and bookmarks |
| Ad-blocking lists, and the sites you allowed ads on | To block ads and trackers as pages load |
| A page you hand off, when you use Handoff (Settings › Handoff, off unless you turn it on) | Written as a file in a temporary folder so it can go to the app you pick; removed at your next handoff, or at once if no app took it. Where Apple Intelligence is on (iOS and iPadOS 26 and later), Apple's model on your device reads the page's title, description, headings and the start of what is under each to tell what kind of page it is and say what it is about; that is not kept, and not sent anywhere. For a video on YouTube the app also reads the video's transcript from YouTube's own page, to put it in the file |
| Files you download | Kept in a temporary folder until you choose where to save them |
| Bookmarks you import | Read from the file you pick, on your device |
| What you type in the address bar, with the names and sites of your tabs and bookmarks and the titles, sites and descriptions of pages in your history, when Apple Intelligence suggests from them; and what you type in History's search when you press Return | Read by Apple's model on your device (Settings › Search › Suggest with Apple Intelligence, on iOS and iPadOS 26 and later where Apple Intelligence is on), to find what you mean; not kept, and not sent anywhere |

iOS and iPadOS may include the app's data in your device's backups, as they do for any app, under your backup settings.

## What leaves your device

Only what using a browser needs:

- **The sites you visit.** Pages load from the sites you open, as in any browser. Those sites see your visits and may collect information under their own privacy policies.
- **Your searches.** Words you type that aren't an address go to the search engine you choose in Settings (Google unless you change it), under that engine's privacy policy.
- **Search suggestions.** With Settings › Search › Search engine suggestions on (it is unless you turn it off), what you type in the address bar goes to that search engine as you type, so it can suggest searches, if the engine offers them (Google, DuckDuckGo and Bing do). An address you type is never sent this way, and the requests carry no cookies or sign-ins. The suggestions from your tabs, bookmarks and the app's commands are made on your device and go nowhere.
- **Sites' icons.** The app downloads the icon a page you visited names, without sending your cookies or sign-ins.
- **Ad-blocking lists.** With ad blocking on (Settings › Ads and trackers, on unless you turn it off), the app downloads the filter lists that are turned on in Settings › Ads and trackers › Filter lists, each from its authors' server (easylist.to, easylist-downloads.adblockplus.org, secure.fanboy.co.nz or raw.githubusercontent.com), at most once every four days, and only if it changed. A request carries no cookies and nothing about you or what you browse; like any server, these see your device's IP address. The blocking itself happens on your device.
- **A page you hand off**, if you turn on Handoff (Settings › Handoff, off unless you turn it on). When you choose Handoff for a page and then pick an app in the share sheet, that app gets a file with the page's text (first, anything you had selected on the page), the page's address and the time the page was read, and a line for your question. The text is the page as it showed to you: if you are signed in to a site, what it shows you alone is part of it. The address goes without the tags that say how you came to the page (`utm_source`, `fbclid` and the like), and the time is by Greenwich's clock, so it does not say which time zone you are in. It goes to the app you pick and nowhere else, under that app's privacy policy, and nothing goes until you pick one. For a video on YouTube the file also has the video's transcript, which the app reads from YouTube's own page. On an iPhone, where YouTube's page shows no transcript, the app loads the video's page at youtube.com a second time, out of sight, as you are signed in or not on that site, reads the transcript there and closes it: YouTube sees that as another visit to the video's page. Nothing is played, and the app asks YouTube for nothing else.
- **Your spaces, bookmarks and tabs, if you turn on iCloud sync** (Settings › iCloud, off unless you turn it on). Each space's name, colour, icon, bookmarks and pinned tabs, and the addresses and titles of your open tabs, are kept in your own iCloud account by Apple (CloudKit's private database), under Apple's privacy policy, so they show on your other iPhones, iPads and Macs. The developer can't read them. Open tabs on sign-in pages, and addresses that carry a sign-in code or token, are left out. Your sign-ins, cookies and what sites store are never synced.
- **Your history, if you turn on History in Settings › iCloud** (off unless you turn it on, and only with iCloud sync on). The pages you went to in each space, when, their titles and descriptions, and the searches you typed in the address bar, are kept in your own iCloud account by Apple in CloudKit's encrypted fields: encrypted in transit and on Apple's servers, and end to end with Advanced Data Protection on. They show in History and the address bar on your other iPhones, iPads and Macs. The developer can't read them. Clearing history on one device clears it on all of them. Turning History off deletes that device's history from iCloud; what your other devices already have stays on them. iCloud keeps no history older than 400 days.

## Permissions

The app asks the system for these only when they are needed, and you can turn each off in the Settings app at any time:

- **Camera and microphone**, when a site you use asks for them, for a video call for example. They go to that site, never to the developer.
- **Files**, only for a file you choose: a bookmarks file to import, or where to save a download.

Passwords, passkeys and verification codes are handled by Apple's Passwords app and AutoFill. Safience does not store them.

## Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports with the developer to help fix problems. You can turn this off at any time.

## Deleting your data

- Remove a space to delete its tabs, bookmarks and website data, its sign-ins included.
- Close a tab to delete its page and its picture. The addresses of the last 25 closed tabs are kept, so you can reopen them.
- Clear History (in History, or Settings › History) to delete your history from the last hour, today, today and yesterday, or all of it. With History on in Settings › iCloud, it is cleared on your other devices and in iCloud too.
- Delete the app to delete everything it kept on that device. Spaces you remove while iCloud sync is on are removed from iCloud too.

## Children

Safience is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, children included.

Senior Product Designer, Web Apps

Seoul, or remote in Asia-Pacific · Design · Full-time

Apply

Mossgrove makes tools for small teams who plan, draw and ship their work in the browser: shared canvases, boards and a design system manager used by studios in 40 countries. We are 70 people, mostly in Seoul, Taipei and Melbourne.

About the role

You will own the design of our canvas editor on the web and on iPad, from first sketch to what ships. You will work with two product managers and a team of eight engineers, and you will spend real time with the studios who use Mossgrove every day.

What you'll do

  • Design new features for the canvas, from flows and prototypes to the last detail of a pointer interaction.
  • Keep our design system healthy: components, tokens and the documentation people actually read.
  • Run research sessions with customers, and turn what you hear into clear decisions.
  • Review work with engineers as it is built, in the browser, on real devices.

What you'll bring

  • Six or more years designing complex web apps, with a portfolio that shows how you think, not only how it looked.
  • Fluency with interaction detail: keyboard, trackpad and touch, on desktop and tablet.
  • Comfort prototyping in code or in a tool that gets close to it.
  • Clear writing in English; Korean or Mandarin is a plus.

Nice to have

  • Experience designing creative tools: canvases, editors, or design software.
  • A habit of shipping small things often.
Pay and benefits ₩95,000,000 to ₩125,000,000 a year in Seoul, adjusted for other locations. Stock options, a yearly learning budget, a home-office stipend and four weeks of leave.

How we hire

A 30-minute call with the hiring manager, a portfolio review with the design team, a paid half-day exercise, and a conversation with two engineers you would work with. We reply to every application within two weeks.
